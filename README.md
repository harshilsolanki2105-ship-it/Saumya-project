## 💡 The Business Concept & Pricing Philosophy

Early-stage startups and consumer brands often build exceptional products but fail due to a lack of distribution and high agency retainer costs.

### 🌟 100% Customized Pricing & Sprints:
> **Our terms depend upon every brand — everything is customizable with NO pre-defined rigid structured plans.** Every partnership is engineered around your unit economics, profit margins, and revenue targets.

**CREATORS® provides a Flexible Performance-First Model:**
1. **Share per Sales (% on Sales Generated)**: Pay only when you generate revenue. **₹0 upfront fixed agency fees**. We take an agreed percentage commission per verified sale/revenue generated.
2. **Strategic Equity Partner (High-Potential Brands)**: When we identify extraordinary potential in your brand, we become your long-term **Branding & Marketing Partner by taking Equity (2%–7%)** — delivering full brand identity, Fractional CMO leadership, pitch deck polishing, and investor access.
3. **Custom Performance Cash Retainer**: Bespoke monthly retainer or milestone fee tailored for funded brands seeking 0% cap table dilution.
