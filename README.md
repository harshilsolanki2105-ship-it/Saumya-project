## 💡 The Business Concept & Pricing Philosophy

Early-stage startups and consumer brands often build exceptional products but fail due to a lack of distribution and high agency retainer costs.

### 🌟 100% Customized Pricing & Sprints:
> **Our prices depend upon every brand — everything is customizable with NO pre-defined rigid structured plans.** Every partnership is engineered around your unit economics, runway, and revenue targets.

**CREATORS® provides a Flexible Dual Model:**
1. **Sweat Equity Partnership (₹0 Upfront Cash)**: CREATORS® invests its growth and creative firepower in exchange for **2%–7% custom advisory equity** (vested via standard FAST/SAFE agreements).
2. **Custom Performance Cash Retainer**: Bespoke monthly retainer or milestone fee tailored for funded brands seeking 0% cap table dilution.
3. **Hybrid Model**: Custom discounted cash fee + 1%–3% micro-equity stake.
