Frequently Asked Questions

Key details on how Share per Sales, High-Potential Equity Partnerships, and custom terms work.

Under our Share per Sales structure, we deploy our complete growth firepower — ad media buyers, funnel designers, copywriters, and video editors — with ₹0 upfront fixed agency retainer. You only pay us an agreed percentage commission on the verified sales and revenue we generate for your brand. Zero sales = zero agency cost to you.

When our venture committee identifies exceptional, high-growth potential in your brand, product, or founding team, we offer to become your long-term Branding & Marketing Partner by taking Equity (typically 2%–7%). In this tier, we act as your Fractional CMO and complete creative studio — leading brand positioning, investor pitch decks, UI/UX, tech PR, and scale strategies.

Every single brand partnership is 100% customizable! We have NO pre-defined rigid packages. Our commission percentages, equity allocations, deliverables, and growth sprints are custom-engineered around your unit economics, profit margins, and revenue milestones.

We utilize standard founder-friendly legal instruments such as the FAST (Founder Advisor Standard Template) agreement or SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity). Equity vests based on performance milestones or over agreed partnership schedules, giving you complete legal protection.

Our team covers 100% of the creative talent, copywriting, high-converting funnel builds, video production, and campaign management. Direct platform ad spend (e.g. ₹15,000 – ₹50,000/mo) is funded by your business, or we focus on viral 100% organic short-form video (Reels, Shorts) and SEO that require zero ad spend.
