Key details on how equity dilution, customizable plans, and legal safety work.

Every single brand partnership is 100% customizable! We have NO pre-defined rigid packages. Our pricing, sweat equity allocation, deliverables, and team composition are custom-engineered around your brand's exact stage, runway, and revenue goals.

We use standard founder-friendly legal instruments like the FAST (Founder Advisor Standard Template) agreement or a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity). Equity vests based on agreed growth milestones or a 12-month period, ensuring you maintain full security.

That is the beauty of our aligned model — we absorb the financial risk with you. You don't owe any back-payments or debt. We only profit when your startup creates long-term real enterprise value.
